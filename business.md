# Poshtichka Business Context

## Company Overview

Poshtichka is a premium interactive event experience based in Burgas, Bulgaria.

Our mission is to create unforgettable memories for every guest through personalized keepsakes printed live during an event.

Unlike traditional guest gifts, every keepsake is personalized and becomes a unique memory that guests take home.

The experience is fun, interactive and memorable.

Guests don't simply receive a gift.

They participate in creating it.

---

# What is Poshtichka?

Poshtichka is a custom-designed vending machine created especially for weddings, corporate events and celebrations.

Each guest receives a special token.

The guest inserts the token into the machine.

The machine prints one personalized item.

The guest chooses what they would like to receive.

Possible products include:

• Personalized postcards

• Temporary tattoos

• Bookmarks

• Printed cards

• Custom branded cards

• Event souvenirs

Everything is designed specifically for each individual event.

Nothing is generic.
